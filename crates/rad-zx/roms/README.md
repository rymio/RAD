# ZX Spectrum ROMs

`48.rom`, `128-0.rom` and `128-1.rom` are copyright Amstrad PLC, who have kindly given their
permission for the redistribution of their copyrighted material with emulators but retain that
copyright. The copyright messages in the ROMs are left intact. The ROMs may not be sold or
embedded in hardware; a product containing them may be sold only if the charge is for the
product, not for the ROMs.

See comp.sys.amstrad.8bit (Cliff Lawson, Amstrad) and the comp.sys.sinclair FAQ:
https://rk.nvg.ntnu.no/sinclair/faq/faqs.html
